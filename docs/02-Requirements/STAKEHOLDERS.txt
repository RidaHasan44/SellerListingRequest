Verified seller:
* Role: Initiates listing
* What they need: Submission, listing status, clear requirements

Marketplace reviewer:
* Role: Reviews listing
* What they need: Violation flags, complete product data, seller verification, eligibility checks

Authenticity reviewer:
* Role: Approves/Rejects luxury items
* What they need: Authenticity request, product evidence, decision history

Marketplace administrator:
* Role: Monitors listings
* What they need: Traceability, policy compliance, exception records

Customer/Buyer:
* Role: Purchases listed items
* What they need: Clear listing information, authentic items, accurate product data, validated seller information
